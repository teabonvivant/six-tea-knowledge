Our study is the first randomized clinical trial of the safety of a GTC containing 400 mgs EGCG administered in divided doses and non-fasted condition for duration of 1 year. One year administration of 400 mgs EGCG administered in a fed state and in divided doses of 200 mgs EGCG twice a day did not produce liver or other toxicities in men at elevated risk for prostate cancer. With the exception of a single grade III nausea reported that may possibly be related to PolyE, no other dose limiting toxicities were observed at this dose and mode of administration.
