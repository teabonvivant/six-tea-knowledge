Autumn tea products showed higher mean concentrations of volatile organic compounds (VOCs), which likely enhanced their more intense aromatic profiles. These findings support the common belief that autumn Tieguanyin tea is superior in aroma, while spring harvests are recognized for their exceptional taste characteristics ( Figure 4 ). Abstract The impact of seasonality on the aroma quality of tea has been documented in various tea types, but not specifically in oolong tea.
