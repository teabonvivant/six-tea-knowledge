品質備註As shown in Fig. 1 and Fig.S1A, the overall traditional sensory evaluation of OT-D was superior to that of OU-D. Both oolong teas exhibited similar appearances, with tightly knotted and slightly curved strands, and uniform leaf bases that were somewhat bright. OU-D had a mellow yet slightly astringent flavor, whereas OT-D exhibited a purely mellow taste. The flavor profiles of the two oolong teas were closely aligned, consistent with the results of the electronic tongue analysis. However, significant differences were observed in the color and aroma of the two teas. OU-D exhibited a light-yellow color with a pure aroma, while OT-D had a deep yellow hue and a complex aroma profile characterized by pure, strong sweet, floral, and fruity aroma.
