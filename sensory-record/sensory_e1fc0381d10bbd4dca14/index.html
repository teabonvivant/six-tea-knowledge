The optimal formulation of IOWT was as follows: sweet osmanthus-towhite tea ratio of 1:5, 4% β -cyclodextrin, 6% erythritol, and 0.5% citric acid. This optimized IOWT formulation obtained the highest sensory score of 89.5 and a TFC of 25.76%. Using ascorbic acid as a positive control, the in vitro antioxidant activities of the optimized IOWT formulation were assessed by measuring its ability to scavenge DPPH radicals, hydroxyl radicals, superoxide anion radicals, and ABTS radicals.
