Specifically, spring teas are characterized by enhanced nitrogen metabolism, which promotes the accumulation of amino acids and certain flavor compounds, whereas autumn teas undergo strengthened carbon metabolism, leading to higher levels of aroma compounds and polyphenols 7 . Oolong tea, a semi-fermented type of tea, is typically produced in spring and autumn 8 . These differential metabolic pathways contribute distinctly to the sensory attributes of the final product: spring oolong teas are often characterized by a delightful and mellow taste, while autumn teas are renowned for their rich and pleasant aroma 8 .
