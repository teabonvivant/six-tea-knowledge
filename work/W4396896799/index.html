首頁研究圖書館White Tea: An Emerging Gift of Nature and Bioprospection Towards the Development of Novel Herbal Formulations, Nutraceuticals, and Cosmetics

White Tea: An Emerging Gift of Nature and Bioprospection Towards the Development of Novel Herbal Formulations, Nutraceuticals, and Cosmetics

Dhrubojyoti Mukherjee、Partha Palit、Shubhadeep Roychoudhury、Shubhajit Halder、Subhash C. Mandal

摘要

Tea is a good friend of the human being as a Nutraceuticals, health drink, and medicinal agent. Different varieties of tea are derived from the pioneer herb, Camellia sinensis. Apart from black and green tea, the silver needle white tea, derived from young leave buds possesses amazing high quality and unique aroma. It is enriched with value-added Phyto marker compounds 404 that elicit multiple health benefits including disease prevention and management of cancer, inflammation, microbial infection and immunodeficiency, and CNS disorders. This is also known as de-stress tea, an ideal drink to refresh the consumers at the optimum level. This silver needle white tea is an antioxidant powerhouse, as well. Among all tea varieties, rarest white tea is the most bioactive health beverage. However, its health benefits and pharmacological activity have not been explored properly, to date. The phytoconstituents variety found to be better than green tea in the context of polyphenols, flavonoids, and saponin contents. Moreover, the yield of bioactive condensed or non-hydrolysable tannin is higher in white tea compared to green tea aqueous extract. Its process of production from young tea leave buds is very emerging. In this regard, the current study will be explored on the importance, area of production, market value, phytopharmaceutical activity on drug and cosmetics aspect, botanical, and taxonomical characterization, production methodology, processing, etc. The study also will be focused on the impact of soil quality, the climate of the cultivating zone and its changing scenario on the production and phytoconstituents yield and composition. This review will also highlight the tissue culture prospect, planting, and nutrient management system of the cultivating area, safe pest management system, processing techniques for extraction of phytoconstituents and their phytochemical characterization by HPLC study, bioprospection of phytopharmacological investigation, pharmaceutical formation development for effective drug delivery especially emphasizing on nanotechnology. The above-cited illustrated items may guide to the researchers and academicians or commercial chemist to develop some potential Phyto pharmaceutically value-added effective herbal drugs and cosmetics formulation and nutraceuticals with great safety and eco-friendly manner.
