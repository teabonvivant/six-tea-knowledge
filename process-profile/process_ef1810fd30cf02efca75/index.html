Abstract Airflow significantly impacts white tea quality by regulating moisture loss and physiochemical reactions. LF-NMR, TEM, SEM, GC-TOF-MS, and UPLC-TQS-MS were used to compare the impacts of withering with different airflows, 0 m/s (AF0), 0.5 m/s (AF0.5), and 1 m/s (AF1), on white tea quality. AF0 impeded moisture loss, accumulating green aroma compounds like ( E )-2-hexen-1-ol, 1-hexanol, and astringents including catechins and caffeine.
