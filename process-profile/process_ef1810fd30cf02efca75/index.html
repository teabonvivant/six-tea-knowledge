Abstract Airflow significantly impacts white tea quality by regulating moisture loss and physiochemical reactions. LF-NMR, TEM, SEM, GC-TOF-MS, and UPLC-TQS-MS were used to compare the impacts of withering with different airflows, 0 m/s (AF0), 0.5 m/s (AF0.5), and 1 m/s (AF1), on white tea quality. AF0 impeded moisture loss, accumulating green aroma compounds like ( E )-2-hexen-1-ol, 1-hexanol, and astringents including catechins and caffeine. White tea ( Camellia sinensis ), a traditional tea category originating from China and characterized by its rich floral aroma, sweet taste, and numerous health benefits, is produced primarily in Fujian Province. The unique processing method for white tea involves withering and drying, and the withering stage is pivotal in shaping its quality characteristics ( Yue et al., 2019 ).
