履歷

Liang Zhang 的身分已由 OpenAlex 與 ORCID 兩個獨立識別來源交叉核對；現有機構資料為 Anhui Agricultural University。已核對的茶研究代表成果包括：Association between chemistry and taste of tea: A review；Tea aroma formation from six model manufacturing processes；LC-MS-Based Metabolomics Reveals the Chemical Changes of Polyphenols during High-Temperature Roasting of Large-Leaf Yellow Tea。此簡介由可追溯資料自動整理，官方個人履歷頁仍另行核對。
