首頁研究人物Zhonghua Liu

Zhonghua Liu

由作品認識研究方向。以下按書目中的作者姓名連接相關文章與公開識別資料。

履歷

Zhonghua Liu 的身分已由 OpenAlex 與 ORCID 兩個獨立識別來源交叉核對；現有機構資料為 Hunan Agricultural University。已核對的茶研究代表成果包括：Microbial bioconversion of the chemical components in dark tea；UPLC–QQQ–MS/MS-based widely targeted metabolomic analysis reveals the effect of solid-state fermentation with Eurotium cristatum on the dynamic changes in the metabolite profile of dark tea；Fungal community succession and major components change during manufacturing process of Fu brick tea。此簡介由可追溯資料自動整理，官方個人履歷頁仍另行核對。

ORCID：https://orcid.org/0000-0003-0000-4565
