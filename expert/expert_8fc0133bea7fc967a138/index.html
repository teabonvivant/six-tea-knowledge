首頁研究人物Fang Dong

Fang Dong

由作品認識研究方向。以下按書目中的作者姓名連接相關文章與公開識別資料。

履歷

Fang Dong 的身分已由 OpenAlex 與 ORCID 兩個獨立識別來源交叉核對；現有機構資料為 Guangdong Food and Drug Vocational College。已核對的茶研究代表成果包括：Formation of Volatile Tea Constituent Indole During the Oolong Tea Manufacturing Process；Formation of (E)-nerolidol in tea (Camellia sinensis) leaves exposed to multiple stresses during tea manufacturing；Does Enzymatic Hydrolysis of Glycosidically Bound Volatile Compounds Really Contribute to the Formation of Volatile Compounds During the Oolong Tea Manufacturing Process?。此簡介由可追溯資料自動整理，官方個人履歷頁仍另行核對。

ORCID：https://orcid.org/0000-0001-8478-433X
