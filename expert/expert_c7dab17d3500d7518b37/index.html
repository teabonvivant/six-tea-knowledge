首頁研究人物Jia Li

Jia Li

由作品認識研究方向。以下按書目中的作者姓名連接相關文章與公開識別資料。

履歷

Jia Li 的身分已由 OpenAlex 與 ORCID 兩個獨立識別來源交叉核對；現有機構資料為 Chinese Academy of Agricultural Sciences。已核對的茶研究代表成果包括：Novel insight into the effect of fermentation time on quality of Yunnan Congou black tea；Rapid determination by near infrared spectroscopy of theaflavins-to-thearubigins ratio during Congou black tea fermentation process；Comprehensive Lipidome-Wide Profiling Reveals Dynamic Changes of Tea Lipids during Manufacturing Process of Black Tea。此簡介由可追溯資料自動整理，官方個人履歷頁仍另行核對。

ORCID：https://orcid.org/0000-0002-9278-0862
