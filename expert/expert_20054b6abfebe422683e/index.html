Wei‐Wei Deng

由作品認識研究方向。以下按書目中的作者姓名連接相關文章與公開識別資料。

履歷

Wei‐Wei Deng 的身分已由 OpenAlex 與 ORCID 兩個獨立識別來源交叉核對；現有機構資料為 Anhui Agricultural University。已核對的茶研究代表成果包括：The profile of dynamic changes in yellow tea quality and chemical composition during yellowing process；Functional Characterization of Salicylic Acid Carboxyl Methyltransferase from Camellia sinensis, Providing the Aroma Compound of Methyl Salicylate during the Withering Process of White Tea；Effects of Three Different Withering Treatments on the Aroma of White Tea。此簡介由可追溯資料自動整理，官方個人履歷頁仍另行核對。

ORCID：https://orcid.org/0000-0003-0732-7076
