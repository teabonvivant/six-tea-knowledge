Weidong Dai

由作品認識研究方向。以下按書目中的作者姓名連接相關文章與公開識別資料。

履歷

Weidong Dai 的身分已由 OpenAlex 與 ORCID 兩個獨立識別來源交叉核對；現有機構資料為 Chinese Academy of Agricultural Sciences。已核對的茶研究代表成果包括：Characterization of white tea metabolome: Comparison against green and black tea by a nontargeted metabolomics approach；Aroma formation and dynamic changes during white tea processing；Metabolomics combined with proteomics provides a novel interpretation of the changes in nonvolatile compounds during white tea processing。此簡介由可追溯資料自動整理，官方個人履歷頁仍另行核對。

ORCID：https://orcid.org/0000-0002-9593-138X
