Jianyu Fu

由作品認識研究方向。以下按書目中的作者姓名連接相關文章與公開識別資料。

履歷

Jianyu Fu 的身分已由 OpenAlex 與 ORCID 兩個獨立識別來源交叉核對；現有機構資料為 Chinese Academy of Agricultural Sciences。已核對的茶研究代表成果包括：Comprehensive analysis of carotenoids constituents in purple-coloured leaves and carotenoid-derived aroma differences after processing into green, black, and white tea；Insights into major pigment accumulation and (non)enzymatic degradations and conjugations to characterized flavors during intelligent black tea processing；Detecting Deep Divergence in Seventeen Populations of Tea Geometrid (Ectropis obliqua Prout) in China by COI mtDNA and Cross-Breeding。此簡介由可追溯資料自動整理，官方個人履歷頁仍另行核對。

ORCID：https://orcid.org/0000-0003-0216-0000
