首頁研究人物Yujie Wang

Yujie Wang

由作品認識研究方向。以下按書目中的作者姓名連接相關文章與公開識別資料。

履歷

Yujie Wang 的身分已由 OpenAlex 與 ORCID 兩個獨立識別來源交叉核對；現有機構資料為 Anhui Agricultural University。已核對的茶研究代表成果包括：Enhanced quality monitoring during black tea processing by the fusion of NIRS and computer vision；Monitoring the withering condition of leaves during black tea processing via the fusion of electronic eye (E-eye), colorimetric sensing array (CSA), and micro-near-infrared spectroscopy (NIRS)；GC–MS, GC–O, and sensomics analysis reveals the key odorants underlying the improvement of yellow tea aroma after optimized yellowing。此簡介由可追溯資料自動整理，官方個人履歷頁仍另行核對。

ORCID：https://orcid.org/0000-0003-3471-2340
