履歷

Yong He 的身分已由 OpenAlex 與 ORCID 兩個獨立識別來源交叉核對；現有機構資料為 Zhejiang University。已核對的茶研究代表成果包括：Rapid Determination of Chlorophyll and Pheophytin in Green Tea Using Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy；Emerging techniques for determining the quality and safety of tea products: A review；Tea saponins: effective natural surfactants beneficial for soil remediation, from preparation to application。此簡介由可追溯資料自動整理，官方個人履歷頁仍另行核對。
