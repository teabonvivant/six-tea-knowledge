中國傳統製茶技藝及其相關習俗

2022 · 拉巴特（摩洛哥） · 聯合國教科文組織保護非物質文化遺產政府間委員會第十七屆常會（17.COM）決定把中國傳統製茶技藝及其相關習俗（項目 01884）列入人類非物質文化遺產代表作名錄。決定正文同時把綠、黃、黑、白、烏龍及紅茶列為六個茶類，並說明種植管理、採摘、手工製作、飲用和分享等實踐。

原文段落 · 4.1 Decision of the Intergovernmental Committee: 17.COM 7.b.2

China's traditional tea processing techniques and associated social practices entail the knowledge, skills and practices around tea plantation management, tea-leaf picking, manual processing, drinking and sharing. Based on natural conditions and local customs, tea producers have developed six categories of tea: green, yellow, dark, white, oolong and black teas. When added to reprocessed teas, such as flower-scented teas, the result is over 2,000 tea products with a variety of colours, aromas, flavours and shapes.
