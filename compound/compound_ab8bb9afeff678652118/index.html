The antioxidant capacity of white tea is lower than that of green and yellow tea ( Table 1 ), likely because of the simple processing steps. The tender shoots of white tea are harvested when most biological activities are being formed and degrading enzymes (polyphenol oxidase and peroxidase) remain active.
